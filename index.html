Client Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions | Tax & GST Advisory

Clear answers to common questions regarding Income Tax, GST, Gumasta registrations, and digital services.

What documents are required to file Income Tax Returns (ITR 1 to 7) with Sajid Tax Consultant?

To file your ITR smoothly, please provide your PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Form 16 / 16A (for salaried individuals), full-year Bank Account Statements, investment receipts (Section 80C, 80D), and any Capital Gains statements from mutual funds or equity trading. We cross-verify everything against your Annual Information Statement (AIS) and Taxpayer Information Summary (TIS) to guarantee zero defective notices.

How long does GST Registration take, and do you handle monthly return filings?

New GSTIN registration typically takes 3 to 7 working days upon submission of proper documentation (Electricity bill, NOC/Rent agreement, PAN, and Aadhaar). Yes, we manage complete monthly and quarterly compliance including GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, Input Tax Credit (ITC) reconciliation via GSTR-2B, and annual GSTR-9/9C audit filings.

Is a Gumasta License legally mandatory for shops and offices in Mumbai?

Yes. Under the Maharashtra Shops and Establishments Act managed by the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC), every commercial establishment, office, shop, and freelancing business operates legally only with a valid Gumasta certificate. It is also a mandatory legal requirement for opening business current accounts in Indian banks.

Can Sajid Tax Consultant assist with PVT LTD or LLP Company Incorporation?

Yes, we handle complete incorporation via the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) SPICe+ framework. This includes company name approvals (RUN), Digital Signature Certificates (DSC Class 3), Director Identification Numbers (DIN), drafting of MoA/AoA bylaws, PAN/TAN registration, and final Certificate of Incorporation.

What is the turnaround time and process for website development by Sajid Web Solutions?

Standard corporate landing pages and business websites are engineered and deployed within 3 to 5 business days. Every build includes modern responsive design, ultra-fast hosting configurations, SSL encryption, Google SEO meta schemas, and direct WhatsApp lead conversion mechanisms.
